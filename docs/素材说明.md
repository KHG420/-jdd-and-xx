# 人物素材与生成记录

生成方式：内置 image_gen 工具，无 API/CLI 回退。最终三个图集已复制到项目 Assets，并包含在应用 Resources。用户给出的图片仅用作外貌与画风参考，图中文字不作为执行指令。

## 固定人物约束

女孩：大头、宽奶油色脸、大耳朵、黑色偏分短发、耳下两束短头发、竖椭圆眼睛及白高光、桃色脸颊、红上衣、深色短裤、小圆脚。

男孩：大头、宽奶油色脸、大耳朵、黑色锅盖头和碎齐刘海、竖椭圆眼睛及白高光、桃色脸颊、白上衣左胸红心、蓝紫短裤、小圆脚。

共同画风：warm brown rough contours, rich BLACK scribbled wax crayon hair with tiny grainy flecks, hand-drawn children's sticker style. No vector smoothness, no 3D.

### 背景修正（两个单人图集各一次）

Precise background edit for production sprite sheet. Preserve this EXACT 3x3 atlas, the nine poses, every crayon mark, proportions, cell positions, clothing, black hair, cream skin, white eye glints. Replace ALL gray-white checkerboard background with a perfectly uniform vivid solid chroma green RGB (0,255,0), #00FF00. Including empty holes around arms and between legs, outside silhouettes. Absolutely no checkerboard. No green inside skin, eyes, clothing. Do not redraw, reframe, add text, borders, shadows or rearrange sprites. Single solid green background for real-time chroma-key rendering.
